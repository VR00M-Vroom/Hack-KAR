# 2026-06-18: Made Version 1 for the front suspension

**Total time spent: 6 hours**

So I made the first version for the front suspension which is basically the same thing as the rear suspension except it has a turning system. I went through many thoughts and ideas but this is the one I settled on. Another potential one I had was to add the turning point after the knuckle but the reason I didn't do that, even though it may have been simpler, was because then the dogbone would be a lot harder to attach to the wheels when those come on. Also this version probably saves more space horizontally.
![image.png](https://cdn.hackclub.com/019ed906-f2ec-7260-8d27-741c33d6a07d/image.png)
## How it works
Ok so when the stick in the center moves the forces the rotary point that are attached to it to rotate since they don't move on the same axis as the center stick. When that happens it makes the bottom link rotate(I modified Vroom's links and added a rotation point in them). The knuckle connects to the top link and also makes that rotate. To account for the changing rotations and positions of the links when the suspension bounces you can see I added a slider to account for the vertical shift and also the arm connected to the link is free to rotate inside the attachment on top of the bottom link. The bottom link is also able to move slightly horizontally. So because of this the moving suspension shouldn't be a problem while turning.
